# tl-parse v0.1 Assurance Argument

## Claim

The exact tl-parse v0.1 candidate is suitable for independent source-release
review as a deterministic, bounded textual MLTL boundary.

## Reasoning

The independently authored dialect maps one-for-one to the pinned tl-syntax
vocabulary. Fail-closed parsing, exact diagnostic goldens, bounded canonical
formatting, generated round trips, malformed corpora, and fuzz seeds address
the declared deterministic failure scenarios.

## Sufficiency Decision

The claim remains open until TM-001 is fully backed, dependency and corpus pins
are current, source and gap reviews have no blocking finding, the shared
assurance chain passes at the candidate revision, and the human owner records a
decision through ix-flow.

## Verification Evidence

Verification evidence for the candidate comes from the shared assurance chain
(`make assurance`): this repository's own producers write structured results,
Quoin seals, retains and verifies them, and Quire exports the static
specification facts. Nothing is retained locally.

This argument previously rested in part on twelve historical evidence records
kept under `evidence/`. Those records were deleted on 2026-09-02 under the
repository owner's release of the evidence-preservation constraint for the
pre-stable phase, recorded in `agent-ix/engineering-assurance#7`. The argument
does not claim them, restate them more weakly, or treat their former existence
as support. The constraint re-applies unchanged when this repository moves
toward stable releases, and evidence retained from that point is immutable.

## Challenges

- Generated and fuzz populations are not exhaustive over all bounded strings.
- The parser does not establish temporal semantic correctness beyond faithful
  construction of the tl-syntax graph.
- Third-party toolchains and the pinned dependency retain their own assurance
  boundaries.
- The `tl-syntax` dependency resolves by exact git revision because that crate
  has no registry release. The earlier expiry condition — "2026-09-07 or when
  the reviewed change merges" — has been met by the merge, and the pin has moved
  from `740182f1` on a pull-request branch to `953ee825` on `main`. Source
  release remains blocked while the dependency has no registry release.
- The Make execution-control guard was removed with the local evidence
  framework. Adding `.IGNORE:` to the `Makefile` makes every recipe report
  success without running, and nothing in this repository inspects Make's own
  controls to notice. The structural backstop — Quoin binds retained inputs by
  digest and every attested result is derived from producer bytes — covers only
  the targets re-run inside `make assurance-inputs`; it does not cover
  `fmt-check`, `lint`, `test`, `check-corpus`, `fuzz-build`, `fuzz-smoke`, `deny`, `audit-unsafe`, `rustdoc`, and the `quire validate` half of `spec`. Tracked as `agent-ix/tl-parse#11`.
- No historical evidence exists for this candidate. The records written during
  early development were deleted under the owner's pre-stable release of the
  preservation constraint, so the only verification evidence is what the current
  chain produces at the reviewed revision. Tracked as `agent-ix/tl-parse#13`.
- Human review and release decision are still open.

Automated evidence may support but cannot close this argument; only the named
human release owner may accept residual risk and approve release.
